## The 4-EMA System (Exact Setup)

| EMA | Color | Role |
|-----|-------|------|
| 200 | 🔵 Blue | Long-term trend. Above = Bullish. Below = Bearish. |
| 48 | 🔴 Red | Pullback/short level. Last line of defense. |
| 13 | 🟢 Green | Pullbacks if above 200, shorts if below 200. |
| 8 | 🟠 Orange | Trend indicator. Follow the 8, follow the money. |

**Entry timeframe:** 2-minute chart
**Trend confirmation:** Watch the 8 EMA on the 5-min and 15-min

### A+ Setup Confluences (90% win rate claim)

The exact trade example from the manual:
1. Above the 200 EMA (bullish trend)
2. 13/48 EMA cross (momentum shift)
3. Bounced off 48 EMA (last defense held)
4. Broke and held Pre-Market High (PMH) as support
5. Reclaimed the 13 EMA with a bull flag
6. Follow the 8 EMA trend all the way up

**Exit:** Follow the 8 EMA trend, hold runners until trend breaks on the 15-minute chart.

## Market Structure — BOS & CHOCH

### BOS (Break of Structure)
- In bearish trend: break of a lower low
- In bullish trend: break of a higher high
- = Trend continuation

### CHOCH (Change of Character)
- After BOS, price reverses and breaks recent LHs or HLs
- Bullish trend → price breaks higher lows = bearish CHOCH
- Bearish trend → price breaks lower highs = bullish CHOCH
- = Trend reversal

### How to Trade CHOCH
1. When CHOCH pattern forms, mark a supply/demand zone based on recent trend
2. Wait for price to retrace to the zone
3. Enter in direction of the trend reversal
4. Stop loss: a few points below the demand zone (break below invalidates — creates new LL)
5. Take profit: when a counter-trend CHOCH forms on chart again

## Supply & Demand Zones — 3 Methods

### Supply Zones (rally-base-drop or drop-base-drop)

| Method | Distal Line (1st) | Proximal Line (2nd) |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Conservative** | Top of wick | Lowest body of base |
| **High-Risk** | Highest wick | Lowest wick |
| **Low-Risk** | Highest wick | Highest body |

### Demand Zones (drop-base-rally or rally-base-rally)

| Method | Distal Line | Proximal Line |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| **Conservative** | Lowest wick | Highest body |
| **High-Risk** | Lowest wick | Highest wick |
| **Low-Risk** | Lowest wick | Lowest body |

## Position Sizing (Exact Rules)

### Daily Sizing
| Day | Size | Reason |
|-----|------|--------|
| Monday | Extremely light | Warming up after weekend |
| Tuesday | Heavier allowed | Golden zone |
| Wednesday | Heavier allowed | Golden zone |
| Thursday | Heavier allowed | Golden zone |
| Friday | Extremely light | Mental battery drained. "Friday is profit taking day." |

### Additional Rules
- **First trade of every day:** Always lightest sizing. It's a warmup. Never go heavy.
- **Within first hour of open:** NOT for small accounts. Always high risk. No trend formed yet.
- **After first hour on Tue-Thu:** Best window for any bigger size.

### Account Building

- $2,500 account = 5 × $500 trades
- Look for 20% gain per trade = $100 profit
- Compound wins — never full port

## Risk Management

### Core Rules
- Risk only what you're comfortable losing
- Use 2:1 or 3:1 risk-reward ratio
  - 2:1 = risk 30%, reward should be 60%
  - 3:1 = risk 30%, reward should be 90%
- Max 3-5 open positions at once
- **NEVER let a green trade go red** (Rule #1)
- Move stop loss to breakeven once trade goes green
- Use trailing stop as trade trends in your direction

### Stop Losses
- Based on personal risk tolerance
- Set below key levels (support/resistance)
- If stop loss hits → accept the small loss
- Never let a small loss become a big loss

### Profit Taking
- Target: 20% per trade
- Start taking profits as early as 5%, should wait for 10%+
- After 20%: sell majority, hold runners for bigger move
- Take profit at key resistance levels (or support for shorts)

## Psychology (from the manual)

### Detach From Fear Of:
- Losing money
- Missing out (FOMO)
- Being wrong
- Leaving money on the table

### Before Every Trade:
1. Know exactly how you're going to enter
2. Know what you'll do after you enter
3. Know how you'll exit
→ This allows you to "trade like a robot"

### Emotional Management:
- Big wins cause emotions → don't trade until euphoria is gone
- Same with losses → step away
- Come prepared 1 hour before market open
- Drink water before market open
- Journal every trade
- Study charts 2 hours outside market hours
- Taking breaks is necessary — trading wears on mental health

### The Middle Phase (Most Important Section):
> "You already know what to do, but you can't get yourself to do it. That disconnect is what destroys most traders."

- Real conviction comes from DATA, not confidence
- Earn it by journaling, backtesting, tracking behavior
- Track behavior, not just PnL
- "Trading isn't about finding certainty, it's about thriving without it"
